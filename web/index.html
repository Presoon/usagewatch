Your account. Every device.

Read quotas directly from provider APIs, including activity on other devices. See session and weekly limits, reset countdowns and credit balances where available. Codex also shows available limit resets.

Provider-reported data. Automatically refreshed.

Keep the limits you need.

Choose which limits appear for each account in both the popup and widget. Expand a card for account details and extra usage. Keep work and personal accounts together, with their own names and sign-ins.

Multiple accounts. Your order. Your limits.

Your keys stay yours.

Connect through provider sign-in or an API key. Tokens and keys are encrypted locally with Windows DPAPI, never saved in plain-text configuration.

Protected by your Windows account.

A widget that fits.

Drag the widget to any of the four screen edges. Hover an account for its quota details, or click to open the popup. Choose a light, gray or dark widget, independently of the app’s light, dark or system theme.

Horizontal or vertical. Always within reach.

Know how fresh it is.

See the next refresh countdown and refresh manually when needed. If an update fails, the last reading stays visible with an outdated-data notice. Provider rate limits show when the app will retry.

Last known usage, with a clear update status.

Ready when you are.

Optionally launch with Windows and choose an automatic refresh interval from 3 to 60 minutes. Disable an account to pause tracking while keeping its saved sign-in, then enable it again when you need it.
